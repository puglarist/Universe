# Universe

## Universe Mega-Patch Iteration: Environmental Interaction & Survival Systems

### Patch 20 – Environmental Interaction & Physics
1. **Object interaction and destruction**
   - Pick up, throw, crush, and interact with cans, crates, barrels, vehicles, doors, and debris.
   - Enable physics-driven destruction including crushing, bending, and breaking.
   - Simulate realistic weight, momentum, and collisions using GPU-accelerated physics.
2. **Scrap and crafting materials**
   - Strip copper, metal, and other materials from vehicles, appliances, and props.
   - Collect scrap for crafting, selling, or building.
   - Use AI to generate realistic scrap placement and density across maps.

### Patch 21 – AI-Generated Realistic Environments
1. **Video-to-world environment generation**
   - Process real-world footage with AI to generate playable 3D zones.
   - Procedurally replicate real city layouts, forests, and rural landscapes.
   - Apply dynamic textures, lighting, and object placement based on video references.
2. **Dynamic map population**
   - Place vehicles, props, wildlife, and NPCs with AI for realism.
   - Integrate player actions (scrapping, hunting, building) into AI world simulation.

### Patch 22 – Survival & Resource Gathering
1. **Hunting and gathering**
   - Spawn animals naturally, with tool-gated hunting and AI-driven stealth/aim mechanics.
   - Allow trees to be cut down for lumber and logs to be processed into planks.
2. **Recycling and junking system**
   - Support exploration of abandoned zones, junkyards, and urban debris fields.
   - Dynamically place valuable scrap, copper wiring, and rare materials with AI.
   - Make crates, vehicles, and appliances interactable for resource gathering.

### Patch 23 – Crafting & Construction
1. **Cabins, houses, and shelters**
   - Build structures from gathered materials, with AI-assisted terrain-aware placement.
   - Keep structures fully destructible with realistic physics responses.
   - Adapt building difficulty and complexity procedurally based on player skill.
2. **Carpentry and vehicle work**
   - Repair, modify, or craft items from wood, metal, and scrap.
   - Add vehicle maintenance, customization, and engine upgrades.
   - Generate parts schematics and predict material requirements with AI.

### Patch 24 – Immersive Interaction Layer
1. **Dynamic environment responses**
   - Make wildlife react to player actions.
   - Simulate interactive trees, foliage, and debris when cut or moved.
   - Preserve realistic vehicle/object destruction and collision behavior.
2. **Procedural AI survival missions**
   - Generate hunting missions, scrap scavenging, logging contracts, and cabin-building challenges.
   - Create dynamic tasks based on world state and player inventory.

### Patch 25 – Integrated Realism
1. **Combined AI maps and survival systems**
   - Translate real-world footage into playable zones for exploration and crafting.
   - Populate NPCs, vehicles, scrap, wildlife, and hazards with AI.
2. **Persistent player impact**
   - Persist world changes like cut trees, built structures, and stripped vehicles.
   - Adjust resources, population, and events based on player actions.

## Outcome
- Universe supports full environmental interaction: pick up, throw, crush, hunt, log, craft, and build.
- Players can scavenge, scrap, and recycle materials for construction and vehicle progression.
- AI transforms real-world video into playable, dynamic maps with procedural population, objects, and challenges.
- Physics, destruction, and crafting systems integrate with AI-generated missions for a reactive survival world.
