# Universe

## Mega-Patch Iteration: Trades, Crafting, and Advanced Professions

This iteration introduces a full production-and-trade gameplay loop covering agriculture, processing, artisan professions, and AI-driven market simulation. It is organized into patches 26–31.

---

## Patch 26 – Agriculture & Resource Production

### Plant cultivation
- Added crop categories: **marijuana, herbs, vegetables, and trees**.
- Added AI-managed growth simulation per plant:
  - watering requirements,
  - sunlight exposure,
  - soil quality and nutrient state.
- Added dynamic environmental modifiers:
  - seasonal growth multipliers,
  - weather impacts (rain, drought, storms),
  - pest pressure affecting health and final output.

### Weighing & selling system
- Harvested crops now support:
  - weighing,
  - packaging,
  - market-ready listing.
- Added two sales channels:
  - player-run stalls/markets,
  - AI-managed trade networks.
- Added legality and enforcement dynamics:
  - product reputation scores,
  - illegal-goods risk values,
  - AI law-enforcement raids and seizure events.

---

## Patch 27 – Weapon & Tool Processing

### Skinning weapons & knives
- Added workflows to remove, clean, and customize blades/firearms.
- Added AI-assisted procedural cosmetics:
  - skins,
  - engravings,
  - modification variants.
- Added physics-aware handling for:
  - sharpening,
  - dismantling,
  - bench-side crafting interactions.

### Forge & metalworking systems
- Added smelting pipeline for ore-to-ingot conversion.
- Added custom fabrication for:
  - tools,
  - blades,
  - vehicle parts.
- Added recipe modes:
  - curated AI recipes,
  - procedural experimentation.
- Craft outcomes now react to **heat, material mix, and weight balance**.

---

## Patch 28 – Artisan & Crafting Professions

### Glass blowing & artisan crafts
- Added craftable decorative/functional glass objects.
- Added AI assistance for:
  - procedural shaping,
  - color blending,
  - fragility simulation.
- Added economy integration so glass goods can be sold, traded, or mission-delivered.

### Carpentry expansion
- Expanded woodworking to build:
  - furniture,
  - cabinets/storage,
  - vehicle interiors,
  - structural enhancements.
- Added AI procedural guidance for material choice, design constraints, and durability tradeoffs.

---

## Patch 29 – Advanced Economy & Trade

### Player-run shops and markets
- Added persistent shop support for crops, weapons, tools, metal goods, and artisan items.
- Added dynamic pricing driven by simulated supply-and-demand signals.
- Added black-market channels with variable risk/reward profiles for illegal goods.

### Weighing, measuring, and grading
- Added quality pipelines for crops, metals, weapons, and crafted outputs.
- Every tradeable good now receives measurable properties and a quality rating.
- AI feedback communicates how quality impacts market value and buyer preference.

---

## Patch 30 – Integration with AI & Multiplayer

### AI procedural missions
- Added mission templates for:
  - farming quotas,
  - smelting contracts,
  - glass commissions,
  - trade-route challenges.
- NPCs and factions can now:
  - buy products,
  - commission custom work,
  - attempt theft/sabotage depending on faction behavior.

### Multiplayer integration
- Added co-op support for farms, forges, and workshops.
- Added persistent cross-session trade networks/marketplaces.
- Added party chat workflows for collaborative production and logistics.

---

## Patch 31 – Environmental & World Realism

### AI reacts to player industries
- Over-farming can now reduce long-term soil fertility.
- Forge/smelting activity creates localized environmental effects:
  - smoke,
  - heat,
  - fire-risk escalation.
- Wildlife behavior now responds to hunting pressure and land-use changes.

### Procedural progression
- Added skill-aware escalation of jobs/challenges.
- As mastery increases, tasks become more complex with higher rewards across:
  - glass blowing,
  - forging,
  - cultivation (including cannabis).

---

## Outcome

Universe now supports a deeply interconnected, skill-based sandbox economy where:
- farming, crafting, smelting, glass artistry, and weapon/tool modification all feed into trade,
- AI governs quality, legality risk, mission generation, and dynamic market behavior,
- multiplayer collaboration is embedded into production and commerce,
- player industry leaves persistent world impact over time.
